# STORY BIBLE — binding. Narration, regions, and forms must agree with this.

## The shape of the story
Seven lights guarded the kingdom. The Shadow took them one by one. Kael —
a small knight with a wolf's heart — frees them region by region, and the
world visibly heals behind him (Terranigma rule). Pip's counted lines are
canon: after Ember "six to go", after Stoneroot "five to go" … 7 total.

## The cast
- **Kael** — the player. Never speaks. Kind first, brave second.
- **Pip** — fox spirit guide. Voice of teaching + warmth.
- **Luna** — the moon spirit, speaks in dreams between regions. Keeper of
  the Den. The 7th light.
- **Grimm** — the antagonist voice. TRUTH (revealed late): Grimm was the
  FIRST guardian — the great wolf whose heart the Shadow took. Every wolf
  form Kael earns is a piece of Grimm's stolen strength finding a new,
  kinder bearer. The final fight frees him; it does not destroy him.
- **The pups** — Grimm's scattered descendants. Every rescue fills the Den
  and (unknowingly) rebuilds the wolf-line the Shadow broke.

1. Ember Hollow — Cinder, flame — FIRE WOLF (ground-slam, burns)  [DONE]

5. Stormreach Cliffs — Aria, sky — STORM WOLF (thunder-dash blink; aura:
   white lightning)
6. Sunken Vale — Meri, water — TIDE WOLF (bubble-shield walk over water;
   aura: droplets)
7. The Shadow Court — Luna's stolen moonlight + GRIMM. Final dungeon wings
   locked by region relics; final boss = Shadow-Grimm, phases demanding
   every form's verb; ending = Grimm freed, Den full, lights restored.
