UPLOADER ATTESTATION — THREE COMMISSIONED NPCS (TRIPO AI)
=======================================================
Recorded: 2026-09-18

The repository owner supplied three Tripo AI text/image-to-3D generations
via chat upload — an old man with a large backpack and staff, a wizard
holding a staff and spellbook, and a cartoon wizard/witch holding a staff
and potions — with a one-line brief for each ("have the wizard replace the
guy who allows you to fast travel. Use the witch as a NPC in the village.
Have the old man be the merchant.") and stated they are his own
generations, made for this project.

This file records that permission in plain words. It is NOT a formal
licence grant: no LICENSE.txt/terms document was supplied alongside any of
the models, and Tripo AI's own terms of service (which would govern
exactly what rights a generation carries) were not independently checked
here.

WHAT WOULD CLEAR IT
Tripo AI's own published terms of service, read and confirmed to grant
commercial/redistribution rights for generations made under a paid or free
account, copied into this directory and replacing this attestation with it
in MANIFEST.json.

WHAT IS SHIPPING FROM THIS FILE
  assets/chars/wayfarer.glb — Tam the Wayfarer's new body (was Wren's own
    rogue_hooded reskin, moon-blue washed)
  assets/chars/merchant.glb — the Den's own shopkeeper (was mage.glb)
  assets/chars/witch.glb    — the Village Square's own settler (was
    another mage.glb reskin)

All three are vendored as supplied — real meshes, ~4,700-4,800 triangles
each, one material, no baked-texture stripping needed since none is
retinted per-instance (each is a one-off face, unlike the mage.glb bodies
they replace, which were shared across many settlers and needed a tint
wash to read as different people). None carries a skeleton — dad's own
words, "these characters don't move, therefore using them as they are
should be fine" — so js/npcs.js's new staticCharacterNpc() places them as
real, still, un-rigged bodies rather than forcing a KayKit rig clip onto a
mesh with no bones for it to bind to.
