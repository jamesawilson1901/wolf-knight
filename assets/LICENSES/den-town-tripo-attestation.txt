UPLOADER ATTESTATION — DEN TOWN MODELS (TRIPO AI)
=======================================================
Recorded: 2026-09-17

The repository owner supplied four Tripo AI text/image-to-3D generations
via chat upload — a quaint wooden tavern, a stone smithy, a wooden
watermill and a flaming stone tower — and stated, when asked, that these
are his own generations, made for this project ("I want to use the fantasy
portal and just recolour it for each one" / "Feel free to use it wherever
you see fit").

This file records that permission in plain words. It is NOT a formal
licence grant: no LICENSE.txt/terms document was supplied alongside any of
the models, and Tripo AI's own terms of service (which would govern
exactly what rights a generation carries) were not independently checked
here.

Two earlier generations in the same upload batch were reviewed and
REJECTED, not vendored: a first tavern attempt (~1.4 million triangles,
thousands of times over this game's per-asset budget, and its topology did
not survive decimation into anything resembling a building — see
design/DEN-REBUILD.md's v1.1 section) and a first watermill attempt (a
whole diorama fused to its own terrain/river/tree base in one mesh, not an
isolated building). Neither of those two files is present anywhere in this
repository.

WHAT WOULD CLEAR IT
Tripo AI's own published terms of service, read and confirmed to grant
commercial/redistribution rights for generations made under a paid or free
account, copied into this directory and replacing this attestation with it
in MANIFEST.json.

WHAT IS SHIPPING FROM THIS FILE
  assets/env/den-town/tavern.glb   — the Tavern (design/DEN-REBUILD.md)
  assets/env/den-town/forge.glb    — the Forge
  assets/env/den-town/mill.glb     — the Mill
  assets/env/den-town/monument.glb — the Monument (new structure, v1.1)

The three buildings are NOT vendored with their original baked textures:
each was converted (assimp glb->obj->glb, dropping the baked material
entirely) to a single flat, textureless material, matching this game's own
asset-reuse-via-tint idiom the same way the dragon egg and the shrine
portal were. No decimation was needed — all four arrived at 3,000-5,000
triangles, already inside budget. The Monument alone keeps its original
baked material (including its lit brazier/lava-crack emissive detail)
since it is a one-off set piece, never retinted per-instance.
