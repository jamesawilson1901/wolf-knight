SKETCHFAB LOW-POLY LIBRARY — UPLOADER ATTESTATION
=================================================
Recorded: 2026-08-19

The repository owner supplied the .glb models listed under this pack in
assets/LICENSES/MANIFEST.json and stated, in their own words, that every one
was "free to use privately and commercially" and that they "personally checked
while downloading."

This file records that permission in plain words. It is NOT a formal licence
grant: the individual per-model Sketchfab licence files (which for many free
Sketchfab models are Creative Commons Attribution / CC-BY, and may therefore
require crediting the original author) were not captured at download. This pack
is therefore PENDING in the manifest until those per-model licence files are
added here. Attribution to the original authors may be required before any
public redistribution.

The models were optimized for the web build with gltf-transform (loader-safe
operations only — texture resize, mesh decimation on high-poly models; no
Draco/meshopt/KTX2, which the game's plain GLTFLoader cannot decode).
